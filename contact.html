FAQ

Who can take part?

While the tournament is made from IB students at EUC Syd, any student who wishes to participate is welcome!

Do I need to bring anything?

Ideally bring water and a pen, however, you both will be provided if forgoten.

What if I can't make a match?

Rescheduling is always available, additionally, it is possible to have an online watch through websites like chess.com upon agreement with the opposing playing.

What are the rules and time limit?

We will be playing classical chess, specifically 60|0.

How do I pay?

To pay, email/message any of the organisers and ask for specific information.
