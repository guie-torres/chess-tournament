Who they are

WFP is the food-assistance branch of the United Nations and the largest humanitarian organization in the world. It was created by the UN General Assembly and the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO), and it is paid for by voluntary donations from governments, organizations, and people like us!

What they do

WFP brings food to people in emergencies such as wars and natural disasters. It also works with communities to improve nutrition and to help people grow and buy their own food. In 2019 it helped 97 million people in 88 countries!

In 2020, WFP won the Nobel Peace Prize for fighting hunger and for working to stop hunger being used as a weapon of war!

How our tournament helps

We support the charity by donating EVERYTHING we make from this tournament. In other words, for each player, we donate 30dkk from the entrance fee!
